# Brand assets

Drop the NeeRav lockups here — they are referenced by the seeded brand kit and
loaded by the Remotion compositions:

- `logo_light.png` — white on transparent, used on photography and dark grounds.
  Recoloured to indigo (`#1A2A46`) automatically on light backgrounds.
- `logo_mark.png` — the tree emblem alone, used wherever the logo renders below
  120px wide.

Both should be PNG with transparency, at least 1024px on the long edge so they
stay crisp at 1080p and 4K.
